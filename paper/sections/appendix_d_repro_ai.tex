\section{Reproducibility, code, and the use of AI}\label{app:repro}

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Every number in this paper is generated. The replays
write machine-readable results (\texttt{make paper-results}). A generator converts
them into the LaTeX tables and into a file of macros from which every in-prose
number is drawn (\texttt{make paper-tables}). The figures are drawn from the same
tables the models read (\texttt{make paper-figures}); the engineering figures and
the macros for every engineering count in the prose are produced by a second
generator (\texttt{python -m paper.figures2}) from the live tables. A number that is not generated
cannot appear in the text, because the corresponding macro is undefined and the
document does not compile. The underlying replays are \texttt{make a1-replay},
\texttt{a2-replay}, \texttt{a4-replay}, \texttt{a5-replay}, \texttt{a1-h1},
\texttt{b0-coverage} and \texttt{mechanisms-coverage}, together with their
full-panel variants. The panel itself is produced by \texttt{make signals} followed
by \texttt{make model-panel}. The source, the decision log and the test suite (591
tests at the time of writing) are available in the project repository.

\paragraph{Data and licences.} AIS positions are from NOAA's Marine Cadastre and
Global Fishing Watch under their respective terms. EIA, FRED, GIE AGSI+ and
Open-Meteo are free public APIs. The World Bank monthly series is licensed CC~BY~4.0
and is the only price series redistributed with the code. The daily TTF series is
obtained from a public quote feed under personal-use terms and is not redistributed;
the loader retrieves it.

\paragraph{Use of AI.} The author used an AI coding assistant (Anthropic's Claude,
through Claude Code) extensively in this project. A substantial fraction of the
pipeline, the modelling harnesses, the tests and the first drafts of this text were
written with its assistance. Every design decision, hypothesis, sign and acceptance
bar in the decision log is the author's. Every result was reproduced by the author
from the stated \texttt{make} targets, and the author takes full responsibility for
the content. The assistant was also used to audit the project's own claims before
this paper was written, which produced the corrections recorded in the final entries
of the decision log. % Author: adjust this wording. It is your statement.
