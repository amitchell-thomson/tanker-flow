\section{Signal definitions and pre-registered signs}\label{app:signals}

Table~\ref{tab:signals} lists the eight tanker signals used in
Section~\ref{sec:partb}, the aggregation applied when collapsing per-terminal or
per-zone bands to one daily series, and the sign fixed before any fit. The sign
convention is as follows. The spread $\text{HH}-\text{TTF}$ is negative in nearly
every period, so a narrowing of the spread means a rise toward zero, which is a
positive change.

\label{tab:signals}
\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.6cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.4cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{8.6cm}c}
\toprule
signal & aggregation & mechanism & prior \\
\midrule
gas in transit, EU-bound & sum over EU zones & more cargo landing in Europe soon; TTF falls & $+$ \\
gas in transit, unresolved & sum & destination unknown; no directional prior & none \\
US loading rate & sum over US berths & gas leaves the US balance (HH firms) and reaches Europe (TTF falls) & $+$ \\
EU discharge rate & sum over EU berths & Europe absorbing gas now; TTF falls & $+$ \\
ballast returning to US & sum & tonnage returning to load; future exports rise & $+$ \\

US load-queue wait & $n$-weighted mean & US cannot export; HH softens and Europe is short & $-$ \\
net export pressure & $n$-weighted mean & $z$(loadings) $-$ $z$(load queue); both components act in the same direction & $+$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table*}

Volumes are in m$^3$ of LNG. Loading and discharge rates are amortised m$^3$ per day
across berth hours, Equation~\eqref{eq:flow}, so that a closed visit integrates to
one cargo. Ages and waits are in days and hours respectively. The project's signal
catalogue documents the remaining twenty-six keys, the two bases, and the confidence
columns (dispersion, open fraction and estimated fraction) that accompany every
value.

\paragraph{A recorded inconsistency.} The catalogue's own text assigned net export
pressure two incompatible signs in two places. The positive sign was adopted on the
economic reasoning above and recorded as the prior. The data returned a negative point
estimate at both horizons with $|t|\le1.6$, which neither confirms nor falsifies it.
